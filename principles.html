Core Web Design Principles

Following a few standard design rules keeps pages easy to scan and prevents clutter.

Design Rule Recommended Standard Why It Matters
Body Font Size At least 16px Prevents users from having to zoom in just to read paragraphs.
Line Spacing 1.5 or higher Gives text breathing room so lines do not blend together.
Color Contrast 4.5:1 ratio minimum Makes sure text stands out clearly against the background color.
Mobile Tap Targets At least 46px tall Makes buttons and menu links easy to tap on touchscreens.
